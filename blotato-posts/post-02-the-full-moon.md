**Slide 2:**
I'd been in Marbella for about two months when I saw the ad. A women's full moon circle. On the beach.

**Slide 3:**
I'd never done anything like it. But something told me to go. Not a voice this time — more like a pull. A quiet knowing that this was meant for me.

**Slide 4:**
We sat together under the moon. We meditated. We wrote down what no longer served us and burned them. For the first time in over a year, I felt something other than grief.

**Slide 5:**
The woman leading the circle was Kim. She was also an energy healer. I booked a session.

**Slide 6:**
She pulled a card to set the intention. The card was The Council of Light. She had never pulled that card before — not in her entire practice. She had to look it up.

**Slide 7:**
I came back the next week. She pulled a card. It was The Council of Light. Again.

**Slide 8:**
She told me: in all her years of practice, she had never pulled the same card twice in a row for anyone. Not for a client. Not for herself. Something was trying to reach me.

**Slide 9:**
By this point, I had started journaling, meditating, doing yoga. I was in Spain with just my son, and for the first time, I had the space to simply be.

**Slide 10:**
Something was opening. Something that had been closed for a very long time was beginning to crack open. → Part 3 coming soon.
