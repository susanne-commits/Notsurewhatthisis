--- CAPTION ---

This was the quiet part of the awakening. No healers. No cards. No sessions.

I spent five months in Spain with just my son. Five months of journaling. Meditating. Walking. Being still.

Five months without managing anyone's schedule, solving anyone's problems, or being the person everyone leaned on.

The silence was loud at first. When you've spent your entire adult life in motion, stopping feels like falling.

What I learned was this: I had been disappearing. For years. The woman who said yes to everything and was never really appreciated for it. The kind of disappearing that happens so slowly you don't notice.

Spain taught me. I want to live for myself and my son. I want to stop abandoning myself to keep everyone else comfortable.

The old version of me didn't die violently. She died the way a season changes. Slowly, then all at once.

When I got back to Virginia, I gutted my closet. My shoes. My clothes. Making space for the woman I had become.

And then I made a decision: I was going back to Europe. This time to Italy. This time to stay.

I am pretty much alone now. But I am not lonely. There is a difference the size of a universe between those two things.
