[Slide 2] I spent five months in Spain with just my son. Five months of journaling. Meditating. Walking. Being still.

[Slide 3] Five months without managing anyone's schedule, solving anyone's problems, or being the person everyone leaned on.

[Slide 4] The silence was loud at first. When you've spent your entire adult life in motion, stopping feels like falling.

[Slide 5] What I learned was this: I had been disappearing. For years. The woman who said yes to everything and was never really appreciated for it. The kind of disappearing that happens so slowly you don't notice.

[Slide 6] Spain taught me. I want to live for myself and my son. I want to stop abandoning myself to keep everyone else comfortable.

[Slide 7] The old version of me didn't die violently. She died the way a season changes. Slowly, then all at once.

[Slide 8] When I got back to Virginia, I gutted my closet. My shoes. My clothes. Making space for the woman I had become.

[Slide 9] And then I made a decision: I was going back to Europe. This time to Italy. This time to stay. → Part 6, the finale, coming soon.

--- CAPTION ---

This was the quiet part of the awakening. No healers. No cards. No sessions.

Just me, a journal, and five months of learning who I am without all the roles attached.

I am pretty much alone now. But I am not lonely. There is a difference the size of a universe between those two things.
