## HOW TO CREATE EACH POST

### Instagram Carousel Posts (Parts 1–6)

Each carousel post has:
1. **Slide 1:** The cover image (cover_X.png) — this is already created
2. **Slides 2–10:** Text slides that need to be created

**Text slide design specifications:**
- Size: 1080 x 1350 px
- Background: Dark navy (#1C1814 or #2C3E50)
- Text color: Off-white/cream (#FAF6F0)
- Accent color: Gold (#C9A96E) for emphasis words
- Font: Cormorant Garamond for body text (if available in your tool), otherwise a clean serif
- Font size: Large enough to read on mobile — approximately 36-42px for body text
- Keep each slide to 2-3 sentences maximum
- Bottom of each text slide: "Part X of 6 | My Awakening Story" in small gold text
- Small gold feather watermark in upper right corner of each text slide

**The text for each slide is in the social-media-content-complete.md file under each Part's "Instagram Carousel" section.**

### Instagram Single Image Posts (Council of Light + Awakening Map)
- Use the cover image as-is (cover_council.png or cover_map.png)
- Post with the caption from the content document

### Facebook Posts
- Use the same cover image as the first photo
- Post the FULL TEXT of the corresponding Part from awakening-story-series.md
- Add the closing hook to the next part (found in social-media-content-complete.md)

THE SHATTERING — Part 1 of My Awakening Story

For most of my adult life, I was the one who held everything together.

I was a school administrator. A mother of three. The person who said yes to everything, showed up for everyone, and made sure the house ran, the kids were good, the family was taken care of. I was the organizer. The fixer. The one nobody worried about because I never gave them reason to.

And then one ordinary morning, while I was drying my hair and getting ready for work, something happened that I still can't fully explain.

A voice — clear, unmistakable, urgent — told me to go talk to my son. Not later. Not after work. Right now. Do not wait. Do it now.

I listened.

And my world fell apart.

What I learned that morning was a family betrayal involving my son that shattered everything I thought I knew about trust and protection. Someone I had welcomed into my home — someone who should have been safe — had not been safe at all. And I had not known.

That is a weight I carry to this day. The guilt of not seeing it. The fury at the betrayal. The grief that I could not undo what had already been done.

We got my son into counseling immediately. I finished the school year. And then I retired.

Not because I was ready. Because I could no longer be the person I had been before that morning. She was gone. The woman who said yes to everything, who held it all together, who never stopped moving long enough to ask if she was okay — she was finished.

I pulled my son out of school and enrolled him in a private online program so he could travel with me. And I made a decision that felt, at the time, like survival more than strategy:

I was going to leave.

Not forever. Not dramatically. But I needed to stop being available to everyone and start being available to myself. For the first time in my life, I needed to be somewhere where nobody needed me to hold anything together — so I could figure out what was left of me.

So I went to Spain. Just me and my son. No plan. No timeline. Just the understanding that if I stayed still any longer, in the life I'd built, I was going to disappear inside it.

I didn't know it yet, but that trip was going to change everything.

Part 2 drops Thursday — I'll tell you what happened under a full moon on a Spanish beach, and the card that had never been pulled before.

If this story resonates, share it with a woman who might need to hear it. 🪶
