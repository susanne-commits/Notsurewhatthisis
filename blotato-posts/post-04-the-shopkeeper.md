After the sessions with Kim, I started wanting crystals. Not decoratively — instinctively. Like my body knew what it needed before my mind caught up.

**Slide 3:**
I found a small shop in my village of San Pedro. When I walked in, the shopkeeper kept looking at me. Not casually. She was studying me. Like she recognized something.

**Slide 4:**
Through my translator app, I told her about the Light Council card — pulled twice in a row. She looked at me in a way I didn't fully understand.

**Slide 5:**
She asked me to come back the following Friday, when her daughter could translate. She asked for my birthday. She said: come back.

**Slide 6:**
When I returned, her daughter was on speakerphone. She explained that her mother felt very called to do a reading for me. She wanted no money.

**Slide 7:**
She laid out her tarot cards. And then she told me I was "very special." That the moment I walked into her shop, she could sense it.

**Slide 8:**
I sat in that tiny office, in a village in Spain, listening to a woman who didn't speak my language tell me things about myself that I was only beginning to see.
