Luma: light. Marinne: the sea.

Born under the full moon, by the Mediterranean, in the middle of starting over.

I am writing this from a villa in the hills of Piedmont, Italy. My son is here with me. The morning light comes through in a way that makes everything feel like it's already been forgiven.

I am not the same person who dried her hair that morning and heard the voice. I am not the woman who flew to Spain carrying a grief so heavy she could barely breathe.

I am someone new. And I built something to hold what I became.

Luma — light. Marinne — the sea. The full moon hanging over the Mediterranean the night I burned the version of myself I no longer wanted to carry.

Everything I create here comes from that. The Still House — because I gutted my home to match my becoming. The guides — because I needed tools for the identity work, the grief, the life redesign.

I didn't build Lumarinne because I had a product idea. I built it because I went through something that cracked me open, and the things that came out needed a home.

If you've found your way here, something in your life may already be shifting. You are not losing your mind. You are waking up.
