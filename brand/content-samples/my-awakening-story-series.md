## What This Series Teaches Us About the Brand Voice

### Subtitle / Hook Style
Each piece uses a two-line hook that:
- Opens with a specific, concrete detail ("What the card meant", "Five months. One journal.")
- Closes with an emotionally charged consequence or revelation
- Never explains too much — creates pull, not push

**Examples to model:**
- "Five months. One journal. And the death of the woman I used to be."
- "Luma: light. Marinne: the sea. Where it all began."
- "The guided journal I wish I'd had when everything broke open."

### Tone Notes from This Series
- Personal and confessional — Susanne is sharing *her* story, not teaching
- Specific and sensory — not abstract concepts, but real moments (a card pulled, a place by the sea)
- Unafraid of weight — "the death of the woman I used to be" is not softened
- Mystery as invitation — subtitles create intrigue without clickbait

### Title Style
- Short. Usually 2–4 words.
- Often a single noun or phrase: *The Becoming*, *The Ankh*, *The Council of Light*
- Feels like a chapter title in a beautiful memoir

## Graphic Card Design Format
*(For recreating this content format)*

- **Background:** Dark, near-black with atmospheric candlelight photography
- **Brand name:** "L U M A R I N N E" — spaced uppercase, small, gold, centered at top
- **Part label:** "PART [N]" — spaced uppercase, gold, centered
- **Divider:** Thin gold horizontal line
- **Title:** Large, bold, white serif (Cormorant Garamond or similar), centered
- **Diamond ornament:** ◆ in gold, centered below title
- **Subtitle/hook:** Italic, gold, centered, 2–3 lines max
- **Footer divider:** Thin gold line
- **Series tag:** "My Awakening Story" in gold italic
- **URL:** lumarinne.com in white, small
