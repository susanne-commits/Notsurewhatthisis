# Brand Photography: The Origin Story
*"Light by the Sea" — Part 6 of My Awakening Story*

## What These Photos Are
These are Susanne's personal photographs from the beach ceremony that became the origin of the Lumarinne brand name.

> **Luma: light. Marinne: the sea. Where it all began.**

The event took place at night on a beach, under what appears to be a blood moon / lunar eclipse. These photos are the authentic source material behind the brand's dark, candlelit visual aesthetic.

---

## The Scene — Visual Reference for Future Content

### Setting
- Beach at dusk transitioning to night
- Blood moon / lunar eclipse visible low on the horizon over the sea
- Open water, flat beach, wide sky — feels vast and still

### The Ritual Setup
- Large mandala-print beach blanket (black and white geometric/tribal pattern) as the altar base
- Wooden round tray at center holding: large glass candle holder, tealight candles, crystals, selenite
- Multiple small glass tealight holders arranged around the tray
- Selenite wands / tuning forks laid around the perimeter
- White open-lid box (appears to be a ritual kit or card deck box)

### Crystals Visible
- Clear quartz cluster (raw)
- Rose quartz (raw chunks and tumbled)
- Black tourmaline (raw)
- Sodalite
- Various tumbled stones in earth tones (carnelian, obsidian, green aventurine, prehnite)
- Selenite wand
- A group of crystals laid on white towel in daylight — feels like a kit or set

### Atmosphere
- Warm amber candlelight against cool blue-grey dusk/night sky
- The contrast of flame against dark creates the exact mood of the brand graphics
- Intimate but not small — feels ceremonial, intentional, significant
- Other people visible in background — this was a group gathering

## The Journal Page — Original Writing by Susanne

Photographed open journal with Susanne's handwriting, on the beach during the ceremony:

> "Tonight's fire is about **LIBERATION**
>
> I am no longer bound by the echoes of old agreements. The fire has transmuted my silence into song, my [silence into truth, my...]"

*(text continues beyond the frame)*

### Why This Matters for Voice
This is Susanne writing in real time, at the moment of origin. The style here is:
- Declarative and present tense ("I am no longer")
- Ceremonial without being performative
- Uses natural elements as metaphor (fire, song)
- "Old agreements" — evocative, non-clinical language for old patterns/conditioning
- Written TO herself, not for an audience — which is exactly why it lands

## How to Use These Photos

These images are Lumarinne's most authentic visual assets. Use them for:
- The "My Awakening Story" series graphics (already in use)
- Email headers for personal letters from Susanne
- Pinterest story pins
- "About" page on lumarinne.com
- Any content about the origin of the brand

### Photography Style to Replicate
When creating new brand photography, match:
- **Time:** Dusk to night, golden hour into dark
- **Light source:** Candles only, warm amber
- **Color palette:** Matches brand exactly — bark brown shadows, gold candlelight, dusty blue sky
- **Objects:** Crystals, journals, candles, natural materials — nothing plastic or modern
- **Mood:** Ritual, ceremony, stillness, intention
- **NOT:** Bright studio light, posed smiling, coffee-shop aesthetic, anything clinical
