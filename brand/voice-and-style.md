# Lumarinne Lifestyle — Brand Voice & Style Guide

## Brand Name
Lumarinne Lifestyle

## Tagline
"Awakening Your Inner Light — Slow, Intentional Guides for Women Ready to Let Go & Begin Again."

## Founder
Susanne

## Website
lumarinne.com

## Brand Mission
Lumarinne Lifestyle creates calm, intelligent, beautifully designed guides for women navigating major life transitions. We help women tend their homes, reclaim their identities, and design lives that reflect who they are becoming — not who they were told to be.

## Core Philosophy
Three cultural philosophies inform everything we create:
- **Wabi-sabi** (Japanese) — Beauty in imperfection. Your life does not need to be perfect to be beautiful.
- **Hygge** (Danish) — Warmth with intention. Creating spaces and rituals that nourish.
- **Lagom** (Swedish) — Enough as abundance. Not too much, not too little. Just right.

## Brand Positioning
Lumarinne occupies the space between clinical self-help and superficial manifestation culture.
- Calm but not passive
- Spiritual but grounded
- Emotional but practical
- Reflective but action-oriented
- Personal but never prescriptive

---

## Voice Character
Lumarinne speaks like a wise friend who has been through her own reinvention and now gently, without judgment, helps other women through theirs.

- **Poetic but not pretentious** — Beautiful language that never feels like it is performing
- **Personal, first-person** — Written by Susanne, signed by Susanne
- **Whispering rather than shouting** — No urgency, scarcity tactics, or aggressive sales language
- **Empowering without being prescriptive** — We offer frameworks, not orders
- **Emotionally honest** — We name hard things without flinching

## Signature Phrases
Use these language patterns to stay on-brand:
- "That is not a small thing."
- "You do not have to fix [X] to begin loving it."
- "This is the beginning of everything."
- "Gently, honestly, at your own pace."
- "Not the Pinterest version. The honest one."

## Words We Avoid
- hustle (used unironically)
- transformation (in unrealistic timeframes)
- limited time / countdown language
- any specific religious references (we are spiritual but non-denominational)

## Writing Rules
- Never use urgency or countdown timers
- Never promise transformation in unrealistic timeframes
- Never shame anyone for where they are
- No aggressive capitalization or exclamation marks in copy
- No specific religion references — spiritual but non-denominational
- Lead with insight or emotion, not product features

## Signature Sign-Off
All letters and personal communications are signed:

> Susanne
> Lumarinne • Awakening Your Inner Light

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
Women 40–65 navigating major life transitions. Intelligent, reflective, and tired of being sold to. They want depth, not gimmicks.

### Life Moments We Serve
- Divorce or separation
- Children leaving home (empty nest)
- Career ending or changing
- Relocation or downsizing
- Spiritual awakening or identity shift
- Retirement and life redesign
- Loss and rebuilding

### What Our Audience Values
- Calm, intelligent tone over hype
- Privacy and self-paced progress
- Beautiful design (they notice quality)
- Authenticity and personal story
- Practical tools with emotional depth
- Permission to start over without shame

### What Turns Our Audience Away
- Aggressive marketing and countdown timers
- Cheesy manifestation language
- Products that feel like therapy worksheets
- Generic Instagram motivational quotes
- Youth-centric messaging

---

## Social Media Tone
Social posts should feel like a quiet conversation, not a sales pitch.
- Lead with insight or emotion, not product features
- Use questions that make women pause mid-scroll
- Share Susanne's personal reflections sparingly but authentically
- Never use "limited time" or artificial urgency

## Hashtags
`#slowliving` `#intentionalliving` `#reinvention` `#womenover40` `#spiritualawakening` `#mindfulhome`

## Content Pillars
- **Insight** — Short reflections on home, identity, and reinvention
- **Permission** — Posts that give women permission to start over, feel lost, or go slowly
- **Product** — Beautiful previews, what's inside, and gentle CTAs
- **Story** — Susanne's personal journey moments (used sparingly for authenticity)
