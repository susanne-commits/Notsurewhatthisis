## Typography

### Display / Headings
**Cormorant Garamond** — Elegant serif with Old Style character.
- Used for: product titles, section headers, quotes, any text that needs to feel literary
- Weight: Regular for elegance, Semibold for emphasis

### Body / UI / Labels
**Outfit** — Clean geometric sans-serif.
- Used for: body copy, navigation, labels, social media captions, any text that needs to feel modern and accessible
- Weights available: Light 200, Regular 300, Medium 500, Semibold 600

### Pairing Rule
The serif headline draws the eye; the sans-serif body provides easy reading at smaller sizes. The contrast creates warmth without sacrificing professionalism.

## Design Principles
- **Generous white space** — Let the content breathe. Pages are never crowded.
- **Subtle accents** — Gold dividers, diamond ornaments, thin accent lines. Never loud.
- **Warm minimalism** — Clean but not cold. Elegant but not sterile.
- **Print-first design** — Everything is designed to look beautiful when printed at home.
- **Dark for premium** — Highest-priced products (Illuminated Year, Second Life, Awakening) use dark backgrounds (`#3D3027` base) to signal premium positioning.

## Visual Style Notes
- Warm photography with natural light
- No harsh shadows or overly polished stock imagery
- Gold (`#C9A96E`) is the signature accent — use it consistently for dividers, ornaments, and highlights
- Diamond ornaments (◆) are a signature brand detail
